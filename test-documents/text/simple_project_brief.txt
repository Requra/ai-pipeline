Project Brief: Online Bookstore Platform

The system shall allow customers to search for books by title, author, and category.
The system shall allow customers to add books to a shopping cart.
The system must support secure checkout using credit cards and PayPal.
Customers shall be able to create an account using their email address.
The system should send an order confirmation email after each purchase.
The platform must load search results in under two seconds for typical queries.
Administrators shall be able to add, edit, and remove books from the catalog.
The system shall keep an audit log of all administrative changes.
