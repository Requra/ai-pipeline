Grandma's Classic Apple Pie Recipe

Peel and slice six large apples and place them in a big mixing bowl.
Sprinkle sugar, cinnamon, and a pinch of nutmeg over the apples and toss gently.
Roll out the pie dough and lay it into a buttered baking dish.
Pour the spiced apples into the crust and dot the top with small pieces of butter.
Cover with a second layer of dough and pinch the edges to seal the pie.
Bake in a hot oven until the crust is golden brown and the filling is bubbling.
Let the pie cool on the windowsill before serving with a scoop of vanilla ice cream.
Enjoy this warm dessert with family and friends on a cozy autumn afternoon.
